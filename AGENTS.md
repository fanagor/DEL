# Writing in DEL

Write for readers of *Differentiable Exact Learning*. Use title case for README headings. Open each section with a question, definition, or explanation that connects the topic to the reader’s task. Introduce each figure, table, and command before presenting the material. Explain the observation a figure supports, the input a command expects, and the result a reader should see. Integrate links into sentences that explain what the destination provides.

Avoid third-person personal, possessive, and reflexive pronouns, including possessive determiners. Name the model, controller, or artifact when a referent could cause ambiguity. Forms of “to be” may introduce definitions, form verb tenses, or serve as passive auxiliaries. Otherwise prefer precise active verbs. Use first-person plural when describing the authors’ work. Avoid em dashes.

Check scientific claims against the released run configurations, evaluation artifacts, extraction settings, and proof domains. Distinguish finite observations, discrete-controller theorems, and assumptions for neural execution. Preserve identifiers, commands, and quotations during prose edits. In the shared paper workspace, also follow the camera-ready conventions in `DELA/AGENTS.md`.
